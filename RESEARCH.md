# RankMe product rationale

Research checked on September 22, 2026. These are public product claims, not an audit of competitors’ private implementations or proof of their results.

## Competitors

[RankYak](https://rankyak.com/) describes a pipeline of site and keyword research, topic clusters, a configurable content calendar, researched articles, internal links, citations, and automatic publishing. Its advertised Pro offer is $99 monthly with 30 articles. It also advertises Search Console-driven refreshes, page checks, backlink exchange, and CMS integrations. Its public pages cannot reveal the exact models, prompts, data suppliers, or review implementation.

[RankPill](https://rankpill.com/) advertises $99 monthly or $990 annually for 30 articles, keyword research, and automation. It presents brand settings, research, writing, images, links, calendars, CMS/webhook publishing, and backlink exchange. Its homepage also promotes Reddit marketing and labels AI visibility tracking as coming soon. These are first-party descriptions; examples and testimonials are not independent validation.

## What RankMe is designed to accomplish

The business objective is qualified search visitors who can become client customers. Publishing is an input, not the outcome. The chosen implementation focuses on the repeatable content operation: understand the business, choose a relevant subject, cover distinct customer questions, support claims with sources, point readers toward an appropriate service, and maintain a steady cadence.

One article every seven days means about 52 per year, averaging 4.3 per month. It is not a strict four-per-calendar-month cap. The twelve-week plan keeps articles connected to one subject. A lower volume makes topic selection and usefulness especially important. Neither publishing frequency nor an AI review score establishes search demand, factual correctness, indexing, rankings, or leads.

## What is included

Local client profiles and history; public HTML inspection; explicit profile confirmation and subject choice; live web research through ChatGPT-authenticated Codex; twelve-week planning; writing, separate evidence review and one automatic repair; metadata and internal links; duplicate-content checks against local article history; brand-matched 3D covers with audience-aware visual review; Markdown, restricted MDX and JSON output; opt-in Git deployment; live page verification; weekly scheduling; local backups and exception recovery.

## Boundaries

This release does not reproduce every competitor feature. It does not include a backlink network, Reddit posting, a paid keyword-volume dataset, rank tracking, Search Console analytics, automated refreshes of published articles, or hosted CMS adapters. A website must have a compatible blog renderer and a configured build/deployment connection. Inspection reads public HTML and a bounded set of discovered pages; it cannot know private business information or render JavaScript-only pages.

No AI API account is required. Codex uses the signed-in ChatGPT subscription, subject to its available features and shared usage limits. RankMe runs locally, while AI inference and web research remain online. The computer must be awake with the server running. Human attention remains necessary for initial business facts, publishing setup, unresolved evidence, authentication failures, and ambiguous deployment recovery.

## Measuring success after connection

Record the site’s starting organic clicks and conversions. Review which article URLs gain impressions, clicks, and enquiries over time using the client’s actual analytics. Prefer topics that support valuable services and real customer decisions. Adjust the subject or improve existing pages when observed results justify it. RankMe deliberately does not invent traffic forecasts or keyword volumes when those measurements are unavailable.
